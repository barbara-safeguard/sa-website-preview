Who it is for

Trusted across faith and education settings

The underlying discipline is the same, clear understanding, good evidence and a lived safeguarding culture. Every session is tailored to your framework and your context.

Religious congregations

Leadership, members, clergy and volunteers, framed around the CSSA eight National Safeguarding Standards and the realities of religious life.

Education settings

Schools, colleges and training providers preparing for Ofsted inspection under Keeping Children Safe in Education, for staff, volunteers and safeguarding leads.

Trustees and governors

Focused briefings for boards and governing bodies, so safeguarding oversight is confident, informed and properly evidenced.

What it can cover

From first awareness to specialist practice

A starting point, not a fixed menu. We agree the content together, pitched to the right level for each group.

  • Safeguarding awareness and induction
  • Roles and responsibilities, for leaders, DSLs and trustees
  • Recognising and responding to concerns
  • Handling a disclosure with care
  • Managing allegations and concerns, and working with the LADO
  • Supporting those who report harm, survivor-centred practice
  • Safer recruitment and vetting
  • Record keeping and evidence
  • Online safety
  • PREVENT and radicalisation
  • Adults at risk
  • Trauma-informed practice
  • Governance and oversight for boards
  • CSSA Standards and Ofsted or KCSIE overviews
