The 8-day safeguarding readiness course

Walk the CSSA eight National Safeguarding Standards, one short lesson a day.

Over eight days I will take you through each of the CSSA National Safeguarding Standards in plain language. Every email is a two-minute read and ends with one simple question to ask of your own setting, so by the end you will have a clear, honest picture of where you stand and where to focus first. It is completely free, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

What you will get, one a day

  1. Leadership, governance and culture, the foundation everything rests on.
  2. Communicating the message, so everyone knows how to raise a concern.
  3. Caring for those who report harm, the heart of safeguarding.
  4. Managing allegations and concerns, a clear trail from concern to action.
  5. Supporting respondents, the standard most settings overlook.
  6. Robust HR and safer recruitment, making your checks watertight.
  7. Training and support, so everyone knows their part.
  8. Quality assurance and improvement, keeping it all getting better.

Plus a welcome to start you off and a short wrap-up with your next step.
