The full toolkits

The complete, ready-to-use resources I build for the settings I work with.

The free readiness scorecards and library give you a real head start. These three toolkits are the complete, tailored versions I develop and hand over as part of an engagement. Because each is built around your setting and carries my full method, they are reserved for clients. Here is a look at what is inside each one. To receive the toolkit that fits you, book a short call and we will talk it through.

The full audit-readiness toolkit

Walk into your CSSA audit or Ofsted inspection calm and evidenced.

What's inside
  • A baseline self-assessment mapped to the eight CSSA standards, or the Ofsted areas with KCSIE, with named evidence against each.
  • An evidence checklist and tracker so nothing is missed and everything is to hand on the day.
  • A prioritised action plan with owners, timescales and RAG status that leaders and trustees can actually use.
  • A trustee or governor dashboard giving a one-page picture of your safeguarding position.
  • An audit-day runbook, so you know exactly what to expect and how to present your evidence.

The complete evidence pack

Turn good practice into evidence trustees and inspectors can see.

What's inside
