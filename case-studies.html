Case studies

Real engagements, told with care and fully anonymised.

Every story below is drawn from real work with religious congregations, education settings and Designated Safeguarding Leads. To protect confidentiality, no client, setting or individual is named or identifiable. What each shows is not only the outcome, but the difference the work made.

1From audit anxiety to a positive CSSA outcome

The situation

A Catholic religious congregation was preparing for its first CSSA baseline audit. The commitment to safeguarding was genuine, but the evidence was fragmented across people and files, and leadership felt real anxiety about whether their practice would stand up against the eight National Safeguarding Standards.

What I did

I carried out an honest baseline review against each standard, built the policies, trackers and a trustee-facing dashboard that were missing, and worked with leadership through a focused Audit Sprint to prioritise the gaps and evidence what was already in place.

The outcome

The congregation reached a positive audit outcome. Just as importantly, leadership described a shift from anxiety to calm, evidenced control, with safeguarding embedded as a lived culture rather than a document to be filed.

The impact, the value delivered

The congregation gained far more than a passed audit. Leadership now sees its safeguarding position clearly at any moment, trustees are given evidence rather than asked to take assurance on trust, and the time and worry that once surrounded audit season have largely gone. Safeguarding moved from a periodic scramble to a steady, embedded part of community life, and the framework we built continues to serve the congregation between audits.

2Beyond compliance, recognised as an exemplar

The situation

A congregation already committed to safeguarding wanted to move beyond meeting the minimum to genuine, demonstrable assurance, and to give its trustees and auditors real confidence in the strength of its arrangements.

What I did

Together we embedded practical toolkits and clear reporting pathways, built a multi-year development plan, and strengthened governance oversight so that evidence mapped directly to each standard and told a coherent story.

The outcome

The congregation was recognised as an exemplar across several key areas in its CSSA engagement, and now sustains that position through regular assurance reporting to leadership and trustees.

The impact, the value delivered

The value was reputational as well as practical. Recognition as an exemplar gave leadership and trustees genuine confidence and a standard for others to look to, the congregation now holds a body of evidence that would withstand any scrutiny, and its safeguarding maturity keeps rising rather than drifting between audits. In short, the work turned a compliance obligation into a source of pride and assurance.

3Mentoring a Designated Safeguarding Lead to confidence

The situation

A newly appointed Designated Safeguarding Lead needed to build capability and confidence quickly, and to prepare their setting for external scrutiny, without a peer nearby to turn to.

What I did

Through structured mentoring, tailored training and hands-on support, I helped the lead develop their policy, casework and reporting skills, and worked alongside them to ready both themselves and their setting for audit.

The outcome

The lead grew into a confident, capable Designated Safeguarding Lead, their setting reached a positive outcome, and they described the support as steady, available and never judgemental, the quiet difference in getting there.

The impact, the value delivered

The real value was lasting internal capability. Rather than depending on outside help indefinitely, the setting now has a confident, competent lead who holds safeguarding day to day, spots and acts on concerns earlier, and carries the culture forward. That is value that keeps paying back long after the engagement ended, and it reduces the setting's reliance, and its risk, over the long term.

4Sensitive case oversight, handled with care

Written in the most general terms, with nothing and no one identifiable.

The situation

An organisation needed sensitive, independent oversight of complex safeguarding matters, where getting the response right mattered deeply to the people affected.

What I did

I provided survivor-centred, trauma-informed case oversight with clear, careful record-keeping, working alongside the relevant statutory partners throughout.

The outcome

Matters were handled with professionalism and compassion, with the dignity and wellbeing of those affected kept at the centre throughout.

The impact, the value delivered

Here the value is measured in trust and dignity. Sensitive matters were handled properly the first time, the people affected were treated with care, and the organisation was given the confidence that it had acted rightly and defensibly by those who needed it most, protecting both individuals and the organisation's integrity.
