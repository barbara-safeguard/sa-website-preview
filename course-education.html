The 8-day inspection readiness course

Walk the safeguarding your setting is judged on, one theme a day.

Over eight days I will take you through the safeguarding themes an inspector looks at, from leadership to the latest Keeping Children Safe in Education, in plain language. Every email is a two-minute read and ends with one simple question to ask of your own provision, so by the end you will have a clear, honest picture of where you stand and where to focus first. One thing worth holding in mind: under the current Ofsted framework, safeguarding is judged simply met or not met, there is no partial pass, so the details are worth getting right. It is completely free, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

What you will get, one a day

  1. Leadership, culture and governance, the foundation everything rests on.
  2. Your Designated Safeguarding Lead, the role at the heart of it.
  3. Safer recruitment and the single central record, the checks that must be watertight.
  4. Identifying and responding to concerns, where daily safeguarding is tested.
  5. Allegations, low-level concerns and the LADO, the harder conversations about adults.
  6. Training and induction, so everyone knows their part.
  7. Online safety, filtering and monitoring, and child-on-child abuse, keeping learners safe.
  8. Learner voice, attendance and continuous improvement, keeping it all getting better.

Plus a welcome to start you off and a short wrap-up with your next step.
