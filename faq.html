Frequently asked questions

A few of the questions leaders and Designated Safeguarding Leads ask most often. If yours is not here, please get in touch.

+Why work with an independent safeguarding adviser?
Independence brings objectivity. As an external Designated Safeguarding Lead I can look honestly at your arrangements, evidence and culture without the constraints of an internal role, and give trustees, leaders and inspectors confidence that your safeguarding has been tested by a fresh, qualified eye. My focus is always on what needs to be done and how to get there, in a way that is practical and proportionate to your setting.
+Do you work with religious congregations or with schools?
Both. I support Catholic religious congregations preparing for and responding to CSSA baseline audits, and schools and education settings preparing for Ofsted inspection under Keeping Children Safe in Education. The underlying discipline is the same, clear policy, good evidence and a lived safeguarding culture, and I tailor every engagement to your framework and context.
+What frameworks do you work to?
For religious life, the CSSA eight National Safeguarding Standards. For education, the Ofsted inspection framework in force from November 2025 and Keeping Children Safe in Education. I also work alongside the RLSS, LADOs, local authorities, children's social care and the Charity Commission as needed.
+How does an engagement usually work?
It starts with a confidential conversation about where you are and what you need. From there I set out a clear, tailored proposal, then we work through a structured journey from baseline review to evidenced assurance. You can see the full journey on the How I work page.
+What is an Audit Sprint?
An Audit Sprint is intensive, focused support before or after an audit or inspection. Together we review your evidence, identify gaps, prioritise risk and build a clear action plan that leaders and trustees can actually use. It is designed to turn audit or inspection anxiety into calm, evidenced control.
+How do you handle confidentiality and sensitive information?
Confidentiality is paramount and non-negotiable. All client and safeguarding material is treated as strictly confidential, and disclosure-sensitive information is handled with particular care. I never publish anything about your setting without your permission, and I do not identify individuals in any public material.
+How are your fees structured?
Every engagement is tailored to your setting, so you receive a clear proposal with the scope and investment set out up front, and no hidden costs. For a sense of what would suit you, please get in touch for a confidential conversation.
+Are you qualified, insured and registered?
Yes. I am an experienced Designated Safeguarding Lead with more than twenty years protecting people across education, faith and mission settings. I hold an enhanced DBS, am registered with the ICO (ZC182781) and carry professional indemnity insurance, and I am a member of the Safeguarding Network and the National Association of Designated Safeguarding Leads.
+How do I get started?
Get in touch through the contact form for a confidential conversation, or book an introductory call or Audit Sprint session directly. I respond personally.
