Independent safeguarding for care providers

Safeguarding that holds across every regulated world you work in

The Care Quality Commission judges safeguarding as its own question, Safe, and under its report cards it is simply met or not met. The Care Act 2014 places clear duties towards adults at risk. And where you care for children, Ofsted and Working Together to Safeguard Children apply too. Holding all of that at once is a great deal. I can help you carry it.

I am Barbara Veeramallay-Permaul, an independent safeguarding consultant and lead. I work alongside care providers, and the faith and charity organisations that run them, to turn safeguarding from something that feels heavy before an inspection into lived, evidenced practice, across both adults at risk and children at risk. That includes care and nursing homes, hospices, supported living, learning disability and mental health services, children's homes and fostering, and the diocesan Catholic care agencies.

Why independent

Safeguarding assurance is not a course you buy and file away. It is a person who has read everything, who welcomes hard questions, and who stays alongside you until the work is done. In a care setting, where the people you support are so often adults or children at risk, that independent eye matters more, not less.
